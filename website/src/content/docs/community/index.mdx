## Asking a good question

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Say what you're trying to do" icon="pencil">
		In one sentence: "an agent should only see tickets of the products they are granted".
	</Card>
	<Card title="Show the policy and the code" icon="document">
		The roles and scopes involved, trimmed, and the check or change that surprised you.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Expected versus what happened" icon="warning">
		The error, or the `Explain` output for a decision you didn't expect.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Versions" icon="information">
		wardn, the store you use, and your database.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

<p class="landing-lead">Never paste real user IDs, customer data, connection strings or secrets: replace them with made-up values. Reading needs no account; to post, sign in with GitHub.</p>

</div>

<div class="landing-section">

## Ground rules

<p class="landing-lead">Be kind and assume good intent: everyone was new to authorization once. Keep each discussion to one topic, and search before you post. Disagree with ideas, not people. If an answer solved your problem, mark it as the answer and vote it up. When a question shows the docs are unclear, the maintainers fix the docs too.</p>

</div>
